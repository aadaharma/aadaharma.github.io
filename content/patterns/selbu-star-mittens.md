## Abbreviations

| | |
|---|---|
| k | knit |
| p | purl |
| MC / CC | main colour / contrast colour |
| k2tog | knit two together (1 st decreased) |
| ssk | slip, slip, knit (1 st decreased) |

## Cuff

1. With MC, cast on 56 (64, 72) sts. Join to work in the round without twisting.
2. Work k2, p2 rib for 6 cm.
3. Knit 1 round, increasing 4 sts evenly: 60 (68, 76) sts.

## Hand

1. Rounds 1–8: work the lice pattern (1 CC, 3 MC) all round.
2. Round 9: start the chart over the first 15 sts for the back of the hand. Work the lice pattern on the remaining sts.
3. On round 14, knit the thumb sts with scrap yarn: 11 (12, 13) sts. Slip them back and knit them again in pattern.
4. Continue until the chart is complete and the mitten reaches the tip of the little finger.

## Top and finishing

1. Decrease at each side every round: k1, ssk, work to 3 sts before the side, k2tog, k1. Repeat until 16 sts remain.
2. Graft the top closed with Kitchener stitch.
3. Pick up 24 sts around the thumb opening and knit 5.5 (6, 6.5) cm. Decrease to 8 sts and draw the yarn through.
4. Weave in the ends and block gently over a damp towel.
